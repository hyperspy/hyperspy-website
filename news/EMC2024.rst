HyperSpy Workshop @ EMC2024, 25th of August 2024
================================================

We are happy to announce a HyperSpy workshop at the `European Microscopy Congress (EMC 2024) <https://www.eurmicsoc.org/emc-2024-report>`_. The workshop will take place on the 25th of August 2024 in Copenhagen, Denmark.
